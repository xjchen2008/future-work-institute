Tom — Salon Front Desk Operator

Tom is a narrow, useful AI operator for hair salons. He answers common questions, captures appointment intent, and hands customers to a human when needed.

What it does

Built for repetitive customer messages

Tom is designed for a narrow front-desk workflow, not a fake all-purpose employee. That makes it easier to deploy, easier to trust, and easier to sell.

Answers common questions

  • Salon hours
  • Basic service questions
  • Location and business info

Handles appointment-intent intake

  • Collects requested service
  • Collects preferred day or time
  • Captures the reason for the message

Escalates safely

  • Hands off when the customer asks for a human
  • Does not fake booking confirmation
  • Does not invent policies or pricing

Example interaction

This is the kind of repetitive front-desk conversation Tom is meant to handle.

Do you have availability Tuesday?
I can help with that. I can take your request for Tuesday and pass it to the salon team. What service are you looking for, and what time works best for you?
Haircut around 3 PM.
Got it — haircut around 3 PM on Tuesday. If you'd like, I can pass that to the salon team now. Would you also like to talk to a human?
