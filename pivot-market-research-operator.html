Operator product

Pivot — Intraday Market Research Operator

Pivot is a narrow day-trading research operator for market prep. It scans movers, organizes watchlists, summarizes setups and risks, and produces a trader briefing before the session.

It is positioned as research support and preparation — not trade execution, financial advice, or guaranteed returns.

What it does

Built for research, watchlists, and premarket prep

The operator helps a trader prepare faster and more systematically by organizing information into a repeatable briefing flow.

Scans the market

  • Premarket movers
  • Relative volume and momentum names
  • News-linked and catalyst-driven setups

Builds a structured watchlist

  • Ranks symbols by setup quality
  • Groups names by theme or risk level
  • Tracks invalidation conditions

Produces a research briefing

  • Setup summaries
  • Risk notes and caution flags
  • Plan-first market prep output
Briefing example

Example of the operator output

The output is meant to be useful before the bell: compact, ranked, and risk-aware.

Top watchlist: 5 names ranked by catalyst, liquidity, relative volume, and intraday clean-setup potential.
Setups: gap-and-go continuation, failed spike fade, and midday reclaim watch with invalidation notes for each.
Risk notes: avoid thin names under the chosen liquidity threshold, respect halts/news volatility, and treat low-float names as higher-risk review candidates.
